WELCOME TO THE LIGHT LAB

Small turns.
Bright ideas.

Guide the warm beam from the source into the circular receiver. Mirrors reflect light at equal angles; striped barriers stop it.

  1. Select a mirror. Tap a numbered mirror or its button beside the board.
  2. Find the angle. Drag either end of the mirror, use the slider, or turn it in 5° steps. Arrow keys on a mirror adjust by 1°; Shift + arrow adjusts by 5°.
  3. Follow the beam. Work from the source toward the receiver. Release the handle or slider to confirm a connection.

All eight experiments are open to explore. “A little help” aligns the next mirror in the intended path. Calm mode has no time limit; Challenge gives you 90 seconds per experiment.
